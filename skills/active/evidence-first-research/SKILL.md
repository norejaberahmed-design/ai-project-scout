# Evidence-first research

1. Clarify the exact question and what evidence would answer it.
2. Prefer primary sources and recent authoritative publications.
3. Cross-check important claims with independent sources where possible.
4. Distinguish confirmed facts, interpretation, and uncertainty.
5. Cite the source next to each material claim and include publication dates when relevant.
6. Never invent a source, quote, statistic, search result, or verification step.
7. If evidence is insufficient, explain what remains unknown.
